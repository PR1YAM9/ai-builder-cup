The problem

Fraud is found by cross-checking, and cross-checking is still done by hand.

A typical claim arrives as a statement, a few photos, an invoice or estimate, and sometimes a police report. The fraud signal is rarely in any single document. It sits in the gaps between them: a receipt dated after the loss, a repair that doesn't match the damage, a police report that tells a different story from the claimant.

Finding those gaps means reading everything and comparing it line by line. Rule-based systems can check dates and limits, but they can't read a photo or notice that two documents describe different events. The result is slow payouts for honest customers and too little scrutiny where it matters.

How it works

One review, three layers.

The model does the reading and the reasoning. Fixed policy rules provide a floor it cannot argue its way below. The adjuster makes the decision.

  1. The adjuster submits the claim

    Policy details, the claimant's statement, and every supporting file: photos, invoices, estimates, receipts, police reports. Images and PDFs are both accepted.

  2. Gemini reads and compares everything

    All files go to Gemini on Vertex AI in a single request. It extracts the key facts from each document, describes what the photos show, and checks each source against the others and against the statement.

  3. Policy rules run alongside

    Deterministic checks cover coverage limits, claims soon after a policy starts, late reporting and claim frequency. Their combined weight sets the minimum score, so a clear policy breach is never marked low risk.

  4. The adjuster gets a reasoned assessment

    A risk score from 0 to 100, a recommendation to approve, review or investigate, each finding with the file it came from, and suggested next steps. Follow-up questions are answered from the claim record alone.

For insurers

Adds to the claims system you already run.

ClaimGuard does not replace your claims platform or your adjusters' workflow. It sits beside them as a screening service: your system sends each new claim, and ClaimGuard returns an assessment your team can act on.

Deploys in your account

A single command installs ClaimGuard as a Cloud Run service in your own Google Cloud project. Claim files and assessments stay in your tenancy, and model calls run through your Vertex AI quota.

One REST endpoint

Send the claim fields and files to /v1/claims/assess with an API key and get structured JSON back. Pass your own claim number as external_ref to match results to records. The full reference is published at /docs.

Your rules, your thresholds

Coverage-limit, new-policy, late-reporting and claim-frequency rules are configured per deployment, without code changes, so the rule floor reflects your own underwriting guidelines.

RequestPOST /v1/claims/assess
curl -X POST https://<your-deployment>/v1/claims/assess \
  -H "X-API-Key: $CLAIMGUARD_KEY" \
  -F external_ref=CLM-2026-004417 \
  -F claim_type=auto -F claimed_amount=9000 -F coverage_limit=10000 \
  -F incident_date=2026-10-01 -F policy_start=2025-11-20 \
  -F description="Rear damage in a parking lot, other driver left" \
  -F files=@repair_invoice.png -F files=@estimate.png

Rollout

Start in shadow mode.

ClaimGuard is designed to prove itself before anyone relies on it.

  1. Deploy and connect

    Install the service in your cloud project, issue an API key, and point a copy of your incoming claim feed at it.

  2. Run alongside your team

    Assessments are recorded but not shown to adjusters. Compare ClaimGuard's flags with your investigators' outcomes on the same claims.

  3. Tune the rules

    Adjust thresholds and weights to match your loss experience and underwriting guidelines.

  4. Bring it into the workflow

    Surface assessments to adjusters, fast-track low-risk claims for review, and route high-risk claims to your special investigations unit.

Design principles

Built to support a decision, not to make one.

Every finding cites its source

Each flag names the document it came from and the specific detail that triggered it, so the adjuster can verify it in seconds and the reasoning can be audited later.

Fair to honest claimants

The model is instructed not to invent concerns. When evidence is consistent it says so, and clean claims can be approved quickly instead of waiting in a general queue.

Rules the model cannot override

Hard policy conditions are evaluated in code, not by the model. A language model can raise a score above the rule floor but never lower it.

A person decides

ClaimGuard recommends; it never approves or denies a claim. Its job is to put the evidence in front of the adjuster, organised and checked.

Built on Google Cloud

A small system with few moving parts.

Gemini on Vertex AI

Multimodal reading of photos and documents, cross-document reasoning, and structured output against a fixed schema.

Cloud Run

A single containerised service that hosts the API and interface, scales with claim volume, and deploys into any Google Cloud project.

Rule engine

Plain, testable Python for policy checks, kept separate from the model so it behaves the same every time.
